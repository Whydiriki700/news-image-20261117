## What the image shows

The front page leads with Ukraine's strike partly collapsing the Crimean Bridge, and carries the day's other consequential stories across regions and fields:

- **Diplomacy**: China confirms a first trilateral summit of Xi, Trump and Putin at APEC in Shenzhen
- **Business**: Nvidia posts bumper results days after Jensen Huang's shock exit, as the US tightens AI chip exports
- **Middle East**: Bahrain intercepts Iranian drones; two more ships hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz
- **Health**: the United States loses its measles elimination status after 26 years
- **Europe**: 146,000 people march in France's second nationwide strike over the cost of living
- **Disaster**: a Russian military plane crash in the Kemerovo region kills all 19 on board
- **Sport**: Belgium rout Italy 4–1 in the Nations League; England end Spain's unbeaten run
- **Also**: Japan scrambles jets after Russian aircraft enter its airspace; four Western nations urge Israel to open Gaza aid crossings

Every story happened, or was first reported, on Tuesday 17 November 2026.

## How it is made

The page is composed programmatically (HTML and SVG with real type, rendered to a 1024 by 1024 PNG), so every headline stays crisp and legible. `main.ts` is the entry program: it validates `entry.png` against the competition's limits (a whole PNG or JPEG, at most 1 MiB and 1024 by 1024 pixels) and copies it to the output directory.

```
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
